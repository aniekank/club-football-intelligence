# Blue Marble

NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, August 2004. Public domain; no attribution
required, though the credit is NASA Earth Observatory.

`blue-marble-8192.jpg` is resampled from the 21600 × 10800 original and encoded
at quality 55 — 3.0 MB. `blue-marble-2048.jpg` (233 KB) paints first and the
large one replaces it in the same GL texture once decoded, because a globe that
arrives three seconds late is worse than a soft globe that is there
immediately. On a slow connection the page simply keeps the soft one.

August rather than the December capture that search results surface first:
December puts North America and most of Asia under snow, which makes the globe
look like a different planet from the one people expect.

Both are served from this origin. Nothing here reaches an external host, which
is what keeps the app inside its content security policy.
